Combination Cheatsheet

How to Play

Goal

Fill every light-blue cell with a digit from 1–9 so that each horizontal and vertical run adds up to its clue. No digit may repeat within a single run.

Reading the grid

  • Light cells — enter your answers here.
  • Dark cells with numbers — clues. The top-left number is the down sum; the bottom-right is the right sum.
  • Plain dark cells — empty blocks that separate runs (not fillable).

Controls

  • Tap a light cell, then tap a number on the pad to enter a digit.
  • Notes — pencil marks for possible values in a cell.
  • Auto Notes — fill every empty cell with its possible values.
  • Clear Notes — remove all pencil marks.
  • Undo — revert your last change.
  • Erase — clear the selected cell (or remove a note in Notes mode).
  • Cheatsheet — valid digit combinations by run length and sum.

Tips

  • Select a cell to highlight its runs.
  • Impossible notes are removed automatically when you place digits.
  • Wrong entries count toward the mistake total shown in the header.
  • Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard, then tap New Game for a fresh puzzle.
